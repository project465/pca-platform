These Rules apply to the recurring payment of the MAT (AI Job Assistant)
pass. Withdrawal of purchase and refunds for other products follow the
[Refund Policy](/legal/refund).

Korean law applies to these Rules. This is also true for foreign residents
in Korea who use the Service. [TO CONFIRM: this sentence is added in the
English draft only. It is not in the Korean text.]

## 1. Who These Rules Apply To

### 1-1. Payment With a Korean Card (KRW)

These Rules apply to MAT passes that personal customers in Korea pay by
card. They pay in Korean won (KRW). The payment service provider is NHN KCP.

A pass that a school or institution gives is not paid automatically.

| Pass | Price | Payment cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Basic 30 days | 9,900 KRW | Automatic payment every 30 days |
| Basic 90 days | 24,900 KRW | Automatic payment every 90 days |
| Researcher 30 days | 19,900 KRW | Automatic payment every 30 days |
| Add-on pass | 4,900 KRW | One-time payment (no automatic payment) |

Recurring payment starts on the day the payment screen shows it for sale. A
period pass paid before that day is not paid again automatically.

### 1-2. Payment With an Overseas Card (USD)

A customer who pays with a card issued outside Korea pays in US dollars
(USD). The payment goes through Paddle. Paddle acts as the Merchant of
Record (the seller of record) for these payments.

## 2. Payment

1. The first payment is made on the day you first pay.
2. After that, the same amount is paid automatically on the same day of each
   payment cycle.
3. The payment screen shows the next payment date and the amount first. The
   payment is made only after you agree.

## 3. Notice Before Payment

1. 7 days before an automatic payment, the Company sends you an email. The
   email shows the payment date, the amount and how to cancel.
2. If the Company raises the price or changes the content, it tells you 30
   days before. It asks for your consent again.
3. If you do not give consent again, the Company does not make the automatic
   payment.

## 4. Cancellation

1. You can cancel at any time with one button in [My Info › Passes] (`내 정보
   › 이용권`). [TO CONFIRM: final English menu label]
2. You can also cancel by customer support email.
3. When you cancel, payments stop from the next payment. You can use the
   period that you already paid for until it ends.

## 5. Refunds

| Case | Refund amount |
|---|---|
| Within 7 days after a payment (including an automatic payment), and you did not use a paid feature in that cycle | Full amount |
| Other requests during the use period | Refund for the remaining days by daily rate, no penalty |
| A reason caused by the Company | Full amount |

1. The paid features are job posting analysis (except 1 free analysis),
   application drafts, interview practice, and file import.
2. The daily rate amount is: payment amount ÷ number of days in the period ×
   remaining days. Any amount below 1 won is dropped (rounded down). For USD
   payments, any amount below 1 cent is dropped. [TO CONFIRM: the Korean text
   only says "원 미만은 버립니다" (amounts below 1 won are dropped). The USD
   rule (below 1 cent) is a draft decision.]
3. The refund is made by canceling the payment on the card you used.
4. For KRW payments, the Company asks NHN KCP to cancel the payment within 3
   business days from the day it confirms the reason for the refund.
   [TO CONFIRM: Paddle terms. For USD payments, write how the Company sends
   the refund to Paddle, and how long Paddle takes.]

## 6. Payment Failure

1. If an automatic payment fails, the Company tells you right away.
2. The Company tries the payment again once a day for 3 days.
3. If the payment still fails, the Company pauses the pass. The Company does
   not charge the unpaid amount (no arrears).
4. If you change the payment card, you can continue using the pass right
   away.
