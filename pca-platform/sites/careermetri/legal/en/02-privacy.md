## 1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

The Company **collects only the minimum information it needs to provide the
Service.** The Company never collects the resident registration number
(주민등록번호) in any case. [TO CONFIRM: decide whether to mention the alien

### 1-1. At Sign-up

| Item | Required? | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| Email, or an ID given by the institution (for example, a student number, 학번) | One of the two is required | To identify the account and to log in |
| Password | Required | To confirm the user. We store it only in a form that cannot be reversed |
| Name, or a display name | Required | To show on the result report |

A user may take the assessment through a requesting institution. In that
case, the Company issues the account by using **the list of names that the
requesting institution gives**. The collected items are the same as above.
The basis for providing them follows the relationship between the requesting
institution and the user, and the consent procedure of the requesting
institution.

### 1-2. When Taking the Assessment

| Item | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Answers to items on a 5-point scale | Scoring |
| Assessment time and the answer time of each item | To continue later, and to check answer consistency |
| Chosen major and department | To give items and results that fit the major |
| Year of study (학년) | Counts by year and interpretation of results |
| Answers to the regional settlement items and the satisfaction items | To calculate performance indicators of the requesting institution |

**Answers to the regional settlement items and the satisfaction items do not
affect the test score.**

### 1-3. When Paying

| Item | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Order number, product, amount, payment status, payment time | To carry out the transaction and keep records that the law requires |

**The Company does not collect or store card numbers, bank account numbers,
card expiry dates or CVC codes.** The payment service provider handles the
payment method information directly.

### 1-4. Information Created Automatically

The Company creates and keeps the access date and time and the service use
records. It uses them for security and to respond to service failures.

### 1-5. When Using the AI Job Assistant (MAT)

| Item | Why we need it | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| The text of a job posting that the user pastes | To find the requirements in the posting and compare them with the user's experience | We delete the text after 1 year |
| Experience notes that the user writes | To turn the notes into options for the experience record | We delete them after 30 days (or when the user saves them as an experience) |
| The content of files that the user uploads (transcripts, certificates of completion, licenses and certificates, awards, activity confirmations and similar files) | To pick courses and activities from the file that the user can write as experiences, and turn them into options | **We do not store the original file.** We discard it right after reading it |
| Drafts taken from a file (names of courses and activities, and the chosen options) | To save them as experiences after the user checks them | We delete them after 30 days |
| A fingerprint of the uploaded file (a hash value that cannot be reversed) and the monthly use count | To avoid reading the same file twice, and to manage the monthly use limit | We delete them after 30 days |
| The content of papers, theses, presentation materials, reports, patents, resumes and personal statements that the user uploads in `Read my materials` (`내 자료 읽기`) | To summarize what the user did and what came out of it, put it in job terms, and pick words to search for job postings | **We do not store the original file.** We discard it right after reading it |
| The summary results of `Read my materials` (title, journal, year, summary, method, results, sentence outline, words to search for job postings, and the author list for a paper) | So the user can see them again and find job postings that match those words | We delete them 1 year after the last use (the user can delete them at any time) |
| A one-page summary that the user sends by pressing `Request a CareerPeak consultation` (`커리어피크 상담 신청`) (material title, summary, method, key results, job terms, matching jobs), and contact details (name, email address, and phone number if given) | To connect the user to a CareerPeak consultation | We delete it 1 year after the consultation ends |
| The record of consent to the consultation request (who sent which items of which material, and when; it does not include the summary text or contact details) | To prove that we received consent | Same as above |

**Names, student numbers, birth dates, grades and other details written in an
uploaded file are part of the file content. They are included in the
overseas transfer in Section 9.** Please cover any information you do not
want to send before you upload. We do not collect the file name. The content
that MAT creates is not used for the test score or the result judgment.

The Company connects a CareerPeak consultation **only when the user asks for
it. The Company asks for separate consent each time the user sends it.** The
Company does not send the original uploaded file (the Company does not store
it either). The Company runs CareerPeak, so this is not a provision to a
third party. The Company receives request emails in its company mailbox
(Section 8). One year after the consultation ends, the person in charge of
the consultation mailbox deletes them.

For a paper, the Company looks up journal indicators (2-year mean citedness,
h-index, citation count). For this, it sends **only the journal name and the
DOI** to OpenAlex (OurResearch, United States), a public academic database.
It does not send names, contact details or the text of the paper.

### 1-6. When Turning On Web Notifications

| Item | Why we need it | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| The notification address and encryption keys of the device where the user turned on notifications (values that the browser creates) | To send MAT's tasks for this week and job posting deadline alerts to that device | We delete them at once when the user turns off notifications or leaves the Service. If the user turns off notifications in the device or browser and they can no longer be delivered, we delete them at the next sending |

Web notifications go only to devices where the user turned them on. For
notifications, we do not collect the device name, browser type or IP address.
Notifications pass through the notification servers of the company that made
the user's browser (Google, Apple, Mozilla and others). **The notification
content is encrypted, so that company cannot read it.** Notifications do not
include the name, score, result or job posting title.

## 2. Purposes of Use

1. To provide the assessment screen, score answers and issue the personal
   result report
2. To make the requesting institution analysis report (**only in anonymous
   group form**)
3. To process payments and refunds, and to keep transaction records
4. To check the reliability and validity of items and calculate norms
   (**only in statistical form that does not identify individuals**)
5. To send notices and answer questions
6. To connect the user to a CareerPeak consultation (**only when the user
   asks for it and gives separate consent**)

**The Company does not use the user's answers for advertising or marketing.
The Company does not sell or provide them to third parties in a form that
can identify an individual.**

## 3. What We Provide to Requesting Institutions

| What we provide | What we do not provide |
|---|---|
| Whether the user took the assessment, and the completion time | An individual's answer to each item |
| Anonymous group results of all test takers | An individual's score and personal result report |
| Distribution by department and by year of study | An individual's answers to the regional settlement and satisfaction items |

**If fewer than 5 people belong to the same value, the item does not show the
number.** A hidden item shows a mark that it is hidden. It is not shown as 0.

## 4. Retention Period and Destruction

When the purpose is achieved, the Company **destroys the personal information
without delay** (Article 21 of the Personal Information Protection Act). The
Company keeps the following records as the law requires.

| Item | Period | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Records of payment and supply of goods | 5 years | Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래법 시행령 제6조) |
| Records of contracts or withdrawal of purchase | 5 years | Same as above |
| Records of consumer complaints or dispute handling | 3 years | Same as above |
| Access records | 3 months | Protection of Communications Secrets Act (통신비밀보호법) |

The one-page summary and contact details sent for a CareerPeak consultation,
and the consent record, are deleted **1 year after the consultation ends**.
If the end date of the consultation is not known, the Company counts 1 year
from the request date.

### 4-1. How We Destroy: We Remove the Person

The two laws pull in opposite directions. The Personal Information Protection
Act requires destruction without delay. The Act on the Consumer Protection in
Electronic Commerce requires keeping payment records for 5 years. So **the
Company does not delete the whole account row. It deletes only the values
that can identify a person, and it keeps the transaction records.** After
this step, the remaining records are not personal information. So the two
laws do not conflict.

**What we delete**

- Email, institution ID, name, password information, password reset tokens
- Affiliation information such as school, year of study and place of
  residence (**only three of these fields together can identify a person**)
- Fields with free text (names of courses and projects, opinions)
- Preferred regions and industries, application and offer history, fit for
  each job posting

**What we keep**

- Payment and refund records (for the legal periods in the table above)
- **Answers and scores that are not linked to a person.** They are the basis
  of the norms. If we delete them, the department counts for that year would
  change later.

The Company keeps a record that the destruction happened. **That record
itself does not contain personal information.**

## 5. Rights of Users and How to Use Them

1. The user may at any time ask to **view, correct, delete, or stop the
   processing of** the user's personal information.
2. Send the request to hari_info@hari.re.kr. The Company acts without delay
   and tells the user the result.
3. A legal representative may use these rights for a child under 14 years
   of age.
4. The Company does not treat the user unfavorably in the Service because the
   user used these rights.

## 6. Children Under 14

CareerMatri is for university students and graduates. **The Company does not
accept sign-ups from children under 14.** If the Company finds that a user is
under 14, it deletes the account without delay. (A service for middle and
high school students is a separate brand. It follows the consent procedure
of a legal representative.)

## 7. Measures to Keep Personal Information Safe

1. The Company stores passwords in a form that cannot be reversed. **The
   Company does not store temporary passwords issued in a batch. If the user
   loses one, the Company can only issue a new one.**
2. The Company limits access to personal information to the persons in
   charge. It keeps access records.
3. The Company encrypts data in transit.
4. Result report notification emails do not contain the result report number
   or the result content. **Other people may see an email while it is being
   delivered.** So the user logs in and checks the result in the user's own
   list.

## 9. Transfer Overseas

The Company transfers personal information overseas as shown below. It does
this **only for users who agreed to AI analysis or file reading** in the AI
Job Assistant (MAT). If the user does not agree, the Company does not
transfer it. Other parts of the Service (assessment, result report,
experience record and others) do not transfer personal information overseas.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Recipient | Anthropic, PBC (United States). Contact: privacy@anthropic.com |
| Country | United States |
| Items transferred | (1) With consent to AI analysis: the text of job postings that the user pastes, and experience notes that the user writes. (2) With consent to file reading: the content of files that the user uploads (including names, student numbers, birth dates, grades and other details written in the file). This also includes the content of papers, presentation materials, resumes and personal statements uploaded in `Read my materials` (including names and contact details written in them). (3) With consent to AI analysis: questions that the user writes in MAT, application sentences that the user asks to edit, written answers in mock interviews, and the parts of the user's data needed to answer them: a summary of assessment results, the experience record and the requirements of the chosen job posting |
| When and how | Each time the user presses `Compare job posting requirements with my experience`, `Move to experience record`, `Ask`, `Fix sentence` or `Check answer`, or uploads a file. Sent over encrypted communication (HTTPS) [TO CONFIRM: final English button labels] |
| Purpose | Analysis to find the requirements of a job posting, to turn notes and file content into options for the experience record, to answer questions, and to return points to fix in application sentences and interview answers |
| Retention and use period | After the recipient returns the analysis result, it does not use the user's content for model training, under the Anthropic Commercial Terms of Service. It deletes the content after the period set in those terms. Section 1-5 shows how long the Company keeps data. Questions written in MAT and their answers are not stored on the Company's server. They stay only on the user's screen |
| How to refuse and the effect | The user can refuse at any time with `Turn off AI analysis` or `Turn off file reading` on the MAT screen. If the user refuses AI analysis, job postings and notes are processed by rules, and use is not limited. If the user refuses file reading, the user cannot upload files. The user can write the same content as a note or by direct input |

The name, email and contact details collected at sign-up, and the assessment
answers and scores, are not transferred.
