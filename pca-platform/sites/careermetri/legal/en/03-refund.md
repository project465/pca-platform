This Policy applies to **personal payments** under the Act on the Consumer
Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래법). Contracts with requesting
institutions follow separate contracts.

Korean law applies to this Policy. This is also true for foreign residents in
Korea who use the Service. [TO CONFIRM: this sentence is added in the English
draft only. It is not in the Korean text.]

## 1. What You Should Know First

**Before payment, you can take the free test (BASIC) to the end and see the
result report.** A paid level (STANDARD or PRO) starts right after payment.
If you upgrade the level in the same attempt after you finish the free test,
the Service does not ask the already answered items again. It opens only the
extra items.

The MAT (AI Job Assistant) pass comes as a 30-day pass and a 90-day pass. One
payment never opens more than 90 days. The pass is a recurring payment
(automatic renewal). The same amount is paid automatically in each payment
cycle. Notices before payment, cancellation, refunds and payment failures
follow the [MAT Subscription Rules](/legal/subscription). An add-on pass is a
one-time payment.

Recurring payment starts on the day the payment screen shows it for sale. A
period pass paid before that day is not paid again automatically.

This free period is a **trial use** under Article 21-2 of the Enforcement
Decree of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce
(전자상거래법 시행령 제21조의2). Article 17(6) of the same Act sets a
condition for a seller who says "withdrawal is limited once the service
starts". The seller must do **both** of these: (1) show that withdrawal is
not possible, and (2) give a trial use. If either one is missing, the limit
itself is not valid.

## 2. Rules for Each Product

| Product | When the service starts | Before that | After that |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assessment ticket | **When you answer the first item** | Full refund, no time limit | No refund |
| Result report upgrade | **When you first open the wider result report** | Full refund within 7 days | No refund |
| Period pass (MAT pass) | Every day, from the moment of issue | Not applicable | **Refund for the remaining days by daily rate, no penalty** (details in Article 5 of the [MAT Subscription Rules](/legal/subscription)) |
| Assessment ticket code | When you register the code and turn it into an assessment | Can be taken back | The Company does not take it back |

### 2-1. Before the Assessment, There Is No Time Limit

The law sets 7 days. But **if you have not started the assessment, the
Company gives a full refund with no time limit.** The reason is that the
Company has not provided anything yet. The Company will not change this rule
to make it narrower.

### 2-2. Why the Upgrade Line Is One Step Later

A person who pays for an upgrade **has already finished the assessment.** If
the rule "no refund after the assessment starts" applied as it is, a refund
would become impossible at the moment of payment. So the Company moved the
line one step later. The line is **when you first open the wider result
report**.

To decide this, **the Company records only the time you first opened it. It
does not record what you read.**

### 2-3. A Period Pass Can Be Divided

A period pass is a continuous transaction (계속거래). So you can cancel it at
any time during the period. **The Company does not charge a penalty.** The
Company returns the full amount for the remaining days.

## 3. Refunds Because of the Company

In the following cases, the Company gives a full refund. This applies no
matter how much you used the product or how long you had it.

1. The paid product is different from what was shown or advertised, or the
   Company did not perform it as the contract says
2. A service failure makes it impossible to take the assessment or see the
   result, and the Company cannot fix it
3. A scoring error produced a wrong result, and a new issue of the result
   does not fix it

In these cases, you can withdraw the purchase within 3 months from the day
you received the product. You can also withdraw it within 30 days from the
day you knew, or could have known, the fact (Article 17(3) of the Act on the
Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).

## 4. How to Apply and How Long It Takes

1. Send your order number and reason to hari_info@hari.re.kr. This
   completes your request.
2. The Company tells you whether a refund is possible and the amount **within
   3 business days** from the day it receives the request.
3. The Company refunds to the original payment method. **Within 3 business
   days** from the day it confirms the withdrawal, the Company returns the
   money or asks the payment service provider to cancel the payment (Article
   18(2) of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).
4. A card cancellation passes through the payment service provider and the
   card company. The Company cannot set how long this takes. If the delay is
   caused by the Company, the Company pays compensation for the delay.

## 5. Cases With No Refund

1. Assessment ticket: after you answer the first item (table in Section 2)
2. Upgrade: after you first open the wider result report (table in Section 2)
3. The Company limited your use because you used the Service in an unfair
   way

**However, even in cases 1 and 2 above, the Company gives a full refund if a
reason in Section 3 (a reason caused by the Company) exists.**
