## Article 1 (Purpose)

These Terms set the conditions and procedures for using the CareerMatri job
fit assessment service (the "Service"). 주식회사 에이치에이연구원 (HA Research
Institute Co., Ltd.) [romanization to confirm] (the "Company") provides the
Service. These Terms also set the rights and duties of the Company and of the
user.

## Article 2 (Definitions)

1. "User" means a member who connects to the Service and uses it under these
   Terms.
2. "Requesting institution" means a university, center, project group or
   local government that asks the Company to give the assessment to its
   students.
3. "Assessment ticket" means the right to take the assessment one time.
4. "Personal result report" means the document that the Company issues after
   it scores the answers of the test taker. "Requesting institution analysis
   report" means the document that counts all test takers of a requesting
   institution together, without names.

## Article 3 (Posting and Changing These Terms)

1. The Company posts these Terms on the Service screen.
2. The Company may change these Terms. When it changes them, it announces
   the start date and the reason. It does this from 7 days before the start
   date. If a change is unfavorable to the user, the Company announces it from
   30 days before the start date. If the user clearly says no to the change,
   the user may end the contract.

## Article 4 (Sign-up and Account)

1. The user applies for membership. The user fills in the sign-up form and
   agrees to these Terms.
2. A user may take the assessment through a requesting institution. In that
   case, the Company may issue accounts in one batch. It uses the list of
   names that the requesting institution gives. **The user must change the
   temporary password at the first login. The Company does not store the
   temporary password.** If the user loses it, the Company can only issue a
   new one.
3. The user is responsible for managing the account and the password. If the
   user lets another person use the account, the Company is not responsible
   for the results.

## Article 5 (Content of the Service)

1. The Company provides the following:
   - Job fit assessment items for each major, and the screen to take the
     assessment
   - Scoring of answers, and the personal result report
   - Anonymous group analysis reports, under the contract with the requesting
     institution
   - Information on companies and organizations in the city of the
     requesting institution, in order of fit (highest fit first)
2. **The Company provides job and company information as a job information
   provider under the Employment Security Act (직업안정법). Its report number
   is J1700020220007.** The Service does not include applying to a company for
   the user, job placement, or writing letters of recommendation.
3. The Company states that the assessment result does not guarantee a job or
   job performance in any specific role. The result measures the user's
   interests and tendencies at the time of answering.

## Article 6 (Nature of Assessment Results)

1. Scoring follows fixed rules that are set in advance. The same answers
   always give the same score. If the user asks, the Company shows the result
   of scoring the same answers again.
2. **The Company does not show a ranking for score differences that are
   smaller than the measurement error.** Jobs with overlapping score ranges
   are shown in the same group.
3. The Company does not use top percentile labels until it has a norm sample.

## Article 7 (Requesting Institution Analysis Report and No Identification of Individuals)

1. The requesting institution analysis report counts all test takers
   together, without names. It does not include the answers or scores of any
   individual.
2. **If fewer than 5 people belong to a value, the report does not show the
   number for that item.**
3. The requesting institution must not try to identify a specific test taker
   by using the analysis report.

## Article 8 (Duties of the User)

The user must not do the following:

1. Take the assessment under another person's name, or have another person
   take it for the user
2. Copy, distribute or send the items, result reports or screens, or make
   derivative works from them (the user may use the user's own result report
   for personal use)
3. Collect items with automated tools, or put a heavy load on the Service
4. Use a weakness of the Service to access data without permission

## Article 9 (Intellectual Property Rights)

1. The Company owns the rights to the items, scoring rules, result report
   format and screen design. The related indicators are registered with the
   Korea Copyright Commission (한국저작권위원회) (C-2025-058658, C-2025-059731,
   C-2025-059732).
2. The user may freely use the user's own answers and the result report
   issued to the user for the user's own career activities.

## Article 10 (Paid Services and Payment)

1. The payment screen shows the types and prices of paid services. The shown
   price includes value added tax (VAT).
2. The amount to pay is fixed at the time of the order. If the price changes
   later, the change does not affect orders that are already made.
3. Contracts with requesting institutions follow separate contracts. The
   withdrawal clauses in these Terms apply to personal payments under the Act
   on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래법).

## Article 11 (Free Use Period)

1. **Before payment, the Company lets the user take the free test (BASIC) to
   the end and see the result report.**
2. This free period is a trial use under Article 21-2 of the Enforcement
   Decree of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce
   (전자상거래법 시행령 제21조의2). A paid level starts right after payment. If
   the user upgrades the level after finishing the free test, the Service
   does not ask the already answered items again.
3. One payment for a MAT (AI job assistant) period pass opens access for up to
   90 days. It is not paid again automatically.

## Article 12 (Withdrawal of Purchase and Refund)

The detailed rules are in the `Withdrawal and Refund Policy`. The main
points are as follows.

| Product | When the service starts | Before that | After that |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assessment ticket | When the user answers the first item | Full refund, no time limit | No refund |
| Result report upgrade | When the user first opens the wider result report | Full refund within 7 days | No refund |
| Period pass | At the moment of issue | Not applicable | Refund for the remaining days by daily rate, no penalty |

**Before the user starts the assessment, the Company gives a full refund with
no time limit.** This is wider than the 7 days in the law. The Company will
not change this rule to make it narrower.

## Article 13 (Suspension of the Service)

1. The Company may stop the Service for a short time. Reasons include
   equipment checks, replacement, breakdown or a communication outage. The
   Company announces planned stops in advance.
2. If a stop happens during the assessment, **the already answered items are
   saved. The user can continue the assessment.**

## Article 14 (Leaving the Service and Limits on Use)

1. The user may ask to leave the Service at any time. The Company destroys
   the personal information without delay. However, the Company keeps payment
   and supply records for the period that the law requires. The Privacy
   Policy has the details.
2. If the user breaks Article 8, the Company may limit the user's use. The
   Company tells the user the reason and the basis.

## Article 15 (Limits of Liability)

1. The Company is not responsible for damage caused by natural disasters, by
   the user's fault, or by illegal acts of third parties.
2. The user may make decisions based on the jobs or the company and
   organization information in the result report. The Company does not
   guarantee or warrant the results of those decisions. The Company does have
   a duty to keep the information accurate.

## Article 16 (Dispute Resolution and Jurisdiction)

1. The Company and the user will discuss in good faith to settle a dispute.
2. If they cannot agree, the case goes to the competent court under the Civil
   Procedure Act (민사소송법).
3. The laws of the Republic of Korea apply to these Terms.
