The programme you are running now

A careers term is planned from two numbers: how many students are in the year group, and where last year's graduates ended up. Headcount does not say what this year's students want. Graduate destinations arrive after the students they describe have already left. So one programme runs for the whole department, and nobody finds out who it was wrong for.

Planning a term today

Headcount and last year

  • One masterclass for the whole year group, because there is nothing to split it by
  • Practitioners invited on the coordinator's own reading of the department
  • Students who find the session is not about their work stop coming, and the office learns that from an attendance sheet
  • An outcome report whose strongest figure is how many attended
Planning a term after one sitting

The distribution, before you book anything

  • Where the year group actually falls across the ten job areas, by department
  • Which sessions the largest groups need, and how many students each one is for
  • Which practitioners to invite, named by the areas that came first
  • The same cohort report as evidence for planning and for outcome reporting

Two students, same department, same year, same 253 items on the same afternoon. One leads on design and development. The other leads on IT convergence and data analysis, and works at almost twice the other's pace. Put them in one session and it is the right session for one of them. Further down this page both of their reports are drawn from real scoring output, side by side.

What running it actually asks of you

To install
Nothing. Students open a link on a phone or a laptop and answers save as they are made
From you
A contact, a rough headcount and the weeks you want it run. That is the whole list
Scope to start
One department, one term. There is no multi-year commitment that has to be signed before it can be judged
Student lists
Optional. A participation link needs no roster; a roster is only for seeing who has not yet sat it
Before you commit
You read your own students' reports and your own cohort report first
How it is priced
Per student, against the size of the cohort. The pilot department is quoted in writing before anything is signed
Start with one department See what comes back

What this does not do: it does not place students and it does not predict who will be hired. It measures which work inside the discipline pulls a student and how they want to do it, then hands the office the distribution while the term can still be changed. Everything after that is the office's own programme.
