What the first year looks like

Two ways in: run it as a university, or operate it in your market as a partner. Either way the split of work is the same, and the first cohort sits before anything is built out.

ACADEMIX provides
  • The instrument, scoring engine and report generation
  • The shared job-area and work-style framework
  • Intake templates for local occupation and employer data
  • Training for the people who run sittings and read results
  • The administrator area for participation and outcome reporting
The local partner provides
  • Translation review by someone who knows the sector
  • Alignment to the national occupational standard
  • Regional employer and institution data, where that module is wanted
  • The relationship with universities and the operation on the ground

How a rollout runs

Week 1 · one callScoping

Which departments, roughly how many students, and whether the regional module is in scope. Twenty minutes is usually enough to know whether this is worth either side's time.

Weeks 2 to 6Pilot cohort

One department sits against a first-pass localisation, and we read the reports together. Nothing is committed until you have seen what your own students got back.

Scoped from the pilotLocalisation build

Translation, standard alignment and employer data, sized against what the pilot actually showed rather than quoted blind.

Department by departmentRollout

The cohort report accumulates from the first sitting, so the second department is planned with evidence from the first.
