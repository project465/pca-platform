What one sitting produces

Two documents. One goes to the student, one to the institution that commissioned the sitting.

Student

Personal report

  • Fitting job areas and work styles inside the discipline
  • Roles to explore first, and a portfolio direction
  • Application, interview and founding strategy
  • A thirty-day action list
Institution

Cohort report

  • Job-area distribution by department
  • Work-style distribution across the cohort
  • Demand for follow-on teaching and mentoring
  • Evidence for programme planning and outcome reporting

Students never see a payment screen. The agreement with the institution creates the seats, and every student in scope receives the full report.
