Why institutions pick this over a generic career test

Engineering-specific, not engineering-adjacent

General career tests stop at "technical field". This one separates mechanical design from structural analysis from process engineering from equipment engineering, because those four hire differently, pay differently and require different software.

Scores you can recompute

Every weight sits in a table, not in code. A department can be handed the formula and arrive at the same 82. A score nobody can reproduce does not survive a procurement review.

Intervals, not false precision

Straight-lining, rushed answers and failed attention checks widen the confidence interval instead of quietly lowering the score. Where two roles overlap, the report says so rather than inventing a rank.

The instrument already travels

The same 250 items exist in Korean, English and Turkish, and the eight activity axes are country-invariant by construction. Localisation replaces the labour-market layer: postings, employers, credentials. The instrument itself stays put.
