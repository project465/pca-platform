The cohort report

Issued to whoever commissioned the sitting: a university, a careers service, a programme office or a regional authority. Responses are aggregated anonymously and no individual is named.

A cell holding fewer than five people shows no number. The moment an anonymous aggregate identifies a person, a coordinator can point at a named student, and then the report cannot be used at all.

Suppressed cells are drawn hatched rather than as an empty bar. An empty bar reads as zero.
