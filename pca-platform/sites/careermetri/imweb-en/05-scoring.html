How it is scored

Groups, not a ranking. When the gap between first and second place is smaller than the measurement error, that order is precision the instrument does not have. Roles whose intervals overlap are reported together as one group.

Scoring is fixed in rules, so the same responses always produce the same scores, and we will re-score a given answer sheet on request. Until a norming sample exists we do not report percentiles.

PropertyWhat it means in practice
Reproducible The weights live in tables, not in code. A coordinator can be shown the same answer sheet re-scored
Interval-aware The interval is computed from the standard error of a 25-item mean, propagated through the conversion matrix, not set as a constant
Within-person Role order is ranked by a student's own highs and lows, not by the raw height of an axis, because axes draw on different numbers of areas
Interest, not ability The 250 items measure what someone wants to do. Held competency comes from evidence a student submits, never from self-rating
