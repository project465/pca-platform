One department, or a whole faculty

A department can start with one year group. A faculty can put several departments under one agreement. Both run on the same instrument and the same scoring. What differs is the size of the roll-up you read afterwards.

Start with one department

A single year group

Enough to see what the reports change before committing further. The coordinator reads the cohort report first and decides what follows.

  • A personal report for every student in the group
  • Anonymised cohort report for the department
  • Roles, portfolio direction, applications, interviews
  • Accounts that outlive graduation
Or a whole faculty

Several departments at once

Every student gets their own report and the institution gets the anonymised roll-up. Where student gaps overlap with employer demand is where the curriculum has work to do.

  • Individual reports for every student
  • Anonymised cohort report: area spread, styles, demand
  • Regional employer matching, where that module is localised
  • Participation and outcome data for reporting

Students never see a price or a payment screen. The agreement creates the seats, and every student in scope receives the full report.
