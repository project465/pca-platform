What travels, and what gets rebuilt

A new country is a content project, not a rebuild. Three layers: one shared worldwide, one rewritten per country, one filled in per university. Knowing which is which is what makes a rollout predictable.

Layer 1 · shared worldwide

Built once, reused everywhere

  • The instrument and its scoring rules
  • The ten job areas and the six work styles
  • The report structure, sections 01 through 08
  • The competency framework behind the strength profile
Layer 2 · rebuilt per country

Where it meets a labour market

  • Translation of every name and passage a person reads
  • Alignment to the national occupational standard, alongside NCS and O*NET
  • Local occupation names, entry routes and credential conventions
  • Regional employer data, where a country wants the retention module
Layer 3 · per university

Supplied by the institution

  • The department list and its own character
  • Employers and institutions around the campus
  • Release policy: who sees results, and when

Because layer 1 is shared, cohorts in different countries stay comparable. That comparability is the asset, and it is why the platform students sit on is one system worldwide even though the marketing sites are separate.

The retention module, where a country wants it

Two items before the sitting and three after, asked in the same words, produce a before-and-after comparison. They are kept apart from the instrument and never enter the scores.

WhenItem · five-point scale
BeforeI intend to work or settle in this region after graduating.
BeforeI know which employers in this region would use my discipline.
AfterHaving seen this, I am more interested in employers and careers in this region.
AfterHaving seen my results, I intend to work or settle in this region after graduating.
AfterHaving seen my results, I know which employers in this region would use my discipline.

Only students who answered both sides are counted. Mixing in those who answered one side would make the two averages belong to different groups of people, and the difference would stop meaning anything.

In Korea, the first market, this module runs against 13,920 employers and institutions across 42 cities, refreshed monthly. Equivalent data for another country is layer 2 work and is scoped with the partner.
