What it measures

Two axes, measured together: which work inside the discipline pulls a student, and how they want to do that work.

LayerScalesItemsHow it is built
Job areas10250 Each area is measured by 25 items. 13 job-interest items and 12 style-bearing items are summed, which holds down the effect of any single item's error
Work styles6120 Independent, collaborative, challenge-seeking, stability-seeking, speed-first, quality-first. Each style is measured by 20 items spread two at a time across the ten job areas, so the same style is asked again in different situations
Consistency33 Checks whether the responses hold together

The 130 job-interest items and the 120 style-bearing items overlap inside the same 250. Every item is self-descriptive and answered on a five-point scale from "not at all like me" to "very much like me".
