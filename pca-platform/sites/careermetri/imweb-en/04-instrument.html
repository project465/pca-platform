What it measures

Two axes, measured together: which work inside the discipline pulls a student, and how they want to do that work. The same 250 items carry both, which is why the sitting is twenty minutes and not two.

10Job areas 25 items each. Summing 25 holds down the effect of any single item's error
6Work styles 20 items each, spread two at a time across the ten areas, so a style is asked again in different situations
3Consistency checks Whether the responses hold together at all

The 130 job-interest items and the 120 style-bearing items overlap inside the same 250. Every item is self-descriptive and answered on a five-point scale.

How a sitting runs

Before the itemsChoose a discipline

The job areas belonging to that discipline are the ones used. A mechanical student is never scored against another field's areas.

About 15 minutesJob-interest items

Interest and fit, area by area. Each answer saves as it is made, so a student can close the tab on a bus and come back that evening.

About 5 minutesStyle-bearing items

Job area and work style measured in the same sentence, which is how the six styles get 20 observations each without adding 120 questions.

ImmediatelyScored

Rules, not judgement. The same answer sheet always produces the same scores, and a coordinator can be shown it re-scored.
