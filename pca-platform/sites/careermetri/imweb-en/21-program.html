The diagnosis is one part of a programme

This is not sold as a test in isolation. The result feeds the lectures, mentoring and recruitment events that follow it, and those are chosen against what the cohort actually scored.

Employment lectures and industry speakers

Role-specific sessions with invited practitioners, chosen against the job areas the cohort scored highest on.

Mentoring across degree levels

Industry-linked mentoring for bachelor's, master's and doctoral students, matched to each student's priority roles.

Career fairs and networking

Recruitment events connected to the employers surfaced by the regional matching, where that module runs.

One-to-one career planning

Individual sessions that start from the report rather than from a blank page, including cover letter and interview review.

An institution can run the diagnosis alone. Most add one or two of these after reading the first cohort report, because the report names which ones would be used.
